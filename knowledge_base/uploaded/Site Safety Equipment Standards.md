# Site Safety Equipment Standards

## Personal Protective Equipment Requirements

Every person present on an active construction site shall wear a certified safety helmet, high-visibility vest and safety footwear with reinforced toe protection at all times. Contractors shall maintain a stock of visitor-sized personal protective equipment at the site entrance for unscheduled inspections. Failure to wear required equipment shall result in immediate removal from the site.

## Scaffolding Inspection Frequency

All scaffolding structures exceeding 2.0 m in height shall be inspected by a competent person before first use, after any alteration, after exposure to adverse weather, and at intervals not exceeding seven (7) days thereafter. Inspection results shall be recorded on a tag visibly attached to the scaffold structure, indicating the date of the last inspection and the name of the inspector.

## Fall Protection Above 1.8 Metres

Any work performed at a height exceeding 1.8 m above the surrounding surface, where no permanent guarding exists, shall require the use of a full-body harness connected to an approved anchor point, or the installation of temporary edge protection with a minimum height of 1.0 m. Anchor points shall be certified to resist a minimum static load of 10 kN.

## Site Lighting for Night Operations

Where construction work continues beyond natural daylight hours, temporary lighting shall be installed to achieve a minimum illuminance of 50 lux across active work areas and 20 lux across pedestrian walkways. Lighting towers shall be positioned so as not to cause glare toward adjacent public roads.

## Emergency Assembly Point Signage

Every construction site exceeding 500 m² in area shall designate at least one emergency assembly point, marked with a visible sign not less than 400 mm by 300 mm, positioned at a safe distance from the site boundary and from any overhead crane operation radius.
